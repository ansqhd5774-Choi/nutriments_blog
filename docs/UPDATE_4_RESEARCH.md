## Evidence corrections
- NIH ODS states there are no formal recommendations for or against probiotic use in healthy people; dose should be strain/product/indication specific.
- CFU is a viable-cell count, not a universal intake recommendation. Higher CFU does not necessarily mean greater benefit.
- Not every fermented food is a probiotic with demonstrated health benefit.
- NCCIH describes efficacy as strain- and condition-dependent with substantial remaining uncertainty.
- FDA has warned about invasive, potentially fatal infection risk in preterm infants receiving products containing live bacteria or yeast.
